**Purpose & Scope**: Defines functional requirements for a European Train Control System to supervise train and shunting movements safely across interoperable application levels.

**Core Functions**:
*   Provide driver information and supervise train movements to enable safe driving.
*   Operate across defined application levels (0, 1, 2, 3, STM) with backward compatibility.
*   Supervise speed and distance against movement authorities and calculate braking curves.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must be functional for train speeds up to 500 km/h.
*   Must be compatible with existing national train control systems without mutual interference.
*   On-board equipment must perform an automatic self-test at start-up.
