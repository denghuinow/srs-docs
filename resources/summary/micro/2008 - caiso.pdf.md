**Purpose & Scope**: Plan to ensure sufficient, located Black Start generators can self-start and energize the grid after a major blackout to enable system restoration.

**Core Functions**:
*   Annually determine required quantity and location of Black Start generators via contingency studies.
*   Test designated Black Start units for capability, including maintaining voltage and frequency within limits.
*   Maintain and annually update a database of all designated Black Start generators.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Black Start units must start and energize a transmission path without external electrical system assistance.
*   Start-up and synchronizing times are capped per technology (e.g., 30 minutes for hydro/gas turbines).
*   The plan and generator test demonstrations must be reviewed/updated at least every five years.
