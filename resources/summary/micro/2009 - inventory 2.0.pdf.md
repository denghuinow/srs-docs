**Purpose & Scope**: System to manage Construction Junction's inventory, tracking items from donation through sale, integrating with QuickBooks POS and the organization's website.

**Core Functions**:
*   Receive donated items into inventory and generate donation receipts.
*   Maintain a categorized inventory of unique and stock items.
*   Process item sales and synchronize inventory with point-of-sale.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must integrate with QuickBooks Point of Sale and Salesforce CRM.
*   User interface must support touch-screen operation.
*   System must be available during all business operating hours.
