**Purpose & Scope**
The system is an e-commerce web application for Marvel Electronics and Home Entertainment to enable online sales, distribution, and marketing of its products.

**Core Functions**
*   Process online purchase of products, including shopping cart and order confirmation.
*   Maintain authenticated customer profiles with order history.
*   Provide product search, detailed categorization, and configurable product options.

**Key Constraints**
*   Must be a web-based product accessible via standard web browsers.
*   Must use secure sockets (HTTPS) for all transactions containing confidential customer information.
*   Must interface with external systems for payment processing, tax calculation, shipping, and CRM.
