**Purpose & Scope**: Provide a single command, control, data receipt, processing, and dissemination system interoperable with present and future tactical unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and specified C4I systems.

**Core Functions**:
*   Command and control of UAV air vehicles and their payloads.
*   Mission planning, including flight route and payload planning.
*   Receipt, processing, and dissemination of payload data and imagery.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must be interoperable with Predator and Outrider UAVs across five defined levels of interaction.
*   Must be capable of entering DII/COE compliant C4I networks.
*   Software must be DII/COE compliant and non-proprietary.
