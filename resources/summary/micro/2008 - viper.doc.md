**Purpose & Scope**
A web-based Supply Chain Management system for Ejada company to coordinate product and service delivery between its coordinators, customers, and suppliers.

**Core Functions**
*   Manage customer and supplier requests (add, view, edit, delete).
*   Manage master data for customers, suppliers, items, and resource locations.
*   Enable suppliers to view requests and submit feedback.

**Key Constraints**
*   Must be a web-based application built with ASP.NET, C#, and MS SQL Server.
*   Must integrate with Ejada's existing .NET framework and two other modules.
*   Must handle at least 100 concurrent users.
