**Purpose & Scope**: Create a proposed plan for a statewide email, messaging, and calendaring service to reduce costs for all executive branch agencies.

**Core Functions**:
* Provide user capabilities to send, receive, and manage email, calendars, and contacts.
* Provide administrative capabilities for account provisioning, archiving, retention, and legal discovery.
* Provide security, anti-virus, and message filtering.

**Key Constraints**:
* The proposed plan must be submitted by December 31, 2009.
* The system must meet federal and state requirements for confidentiality, privacy, and security.
* Agency migration must be completed by June 30, 2013.
