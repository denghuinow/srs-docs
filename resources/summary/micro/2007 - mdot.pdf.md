**Purpose & Scope**: A prototype research system to evaluate the utility of Vehicle Infrastructure Integration (VII) data for enhancing transportation safety, mobility, and asset management in Michigan.

**Core Functions**:
*   Collect probe vehicle, traffic, and weather data from multiple sources.
*   Process collected data to derive traffic conditions, incidents, and road surface information.
*   Publish analyzed data and alerts to external systems and human operators.

**Key Constraints**:
*   The system is a prototype, not intended for operational use.
*   Must handle VII data which is "near real-time" and of variable quality.
*   Must conform to Michigan Department of Information Technology (MDIT) standards.
