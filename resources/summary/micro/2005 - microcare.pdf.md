**Purpose & Scope**: Automate the voucher management for a one-year STD treatment pilot program in Mbarara District, handling voucher lifecycle from creation to provider reimbursement.

**Core Functions**:
*   Create and track uniquely identified, barcoded vouchers.
*   Process and validate medical claims submitted by service providers.
*   Manage distributor sales and returns of vouchers.

**Key Constraints**:
*   System must interface with barcode and thumbprint readers.
*   Vouchers are sold only in pairs (client/partner) and one at a time per person.
*   The database design must support expansion beyond the initial 20,000 vouchers.
