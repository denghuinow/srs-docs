**Purpose & Scope**: A digital radio system for European railways to provide interoperable ground-train and ground-based mobile voice and data communications.

**Core Functions**:
*   Support mandatory voice services (point-to-point, group, broadcast, emergency calls).
*   Provide data bearer services for train control (ERTMS/ETCS) and other applications.
*   Enable railway-specific addressing (functional and location-dependent).

**Key Constraints**:
*   System must support train speeds up to 500 km/h.
*   Mandatory features must be implemented for interoperability.
*   Network must achieve defined call set-up times (e.g., <2s for Railway emergency calls) in 95% of cases.
