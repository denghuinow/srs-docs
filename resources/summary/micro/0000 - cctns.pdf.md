**Purpose & Scope**: A national system to improve crime investigation and criminal detection for police personnel and citizens.

**Core Functions**:
*   Register citizen complaints.
*   Manage the investigation process.
*   Search for cases, persons, and property.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must maintain an unalterable audit trail for all critical actions.
*   Must provide role-based access control and security.
*   Must be available during specified hours with defined maximum downtime.
