**Purpose & Scope**: The NDE system receives, processes, archives, and distributes environmental satellite data from NPP and NPOESS missions to NOAA's operational and climate communities.

**Core Functions**:
* Generate and disseminate NOAA-unique and tailored data products.
* Archive NOAA-unique products to the CLASS long-term archive.
* Distribute data products to authorized users.

**Key Constraints**:
* Must comply with Federal Enterprise Architecture and ESPC security procedures.
* System availability for ingest, generation, and distribution shall not be interrupted for more than 2 hours in any 24-hour period.
* Must support product volumes of up to 8 TB/day input and 16 TB/day output during NPOESS missions.
