**Purpose & Scope**: The website aims to attract Oregon high school students to pursue computer science degrees within the Oregon University System.

**Core Functions**:
*   Provide information on CS careers, college programs, and professional resources.
*   Feature profiles of real people in diverse CS fields.
*   Include a search function for internal and external CS-related information.

**Key Constraints**:
*   The site must be hosted on and consistent with the OUS domain and servers.
*   Design must accommodate limited bandwidth for video streaming.
*   Development is constrained by limited Chancellor's Office resources.
