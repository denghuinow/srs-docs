**Purpose & Scope**: Define a standardized communication system for data transfer between wind turbine controllers and remote SCADA systems, applicable to single turbines and wind farms.

**Core Functions**:
*   Transfer of operational data (measurements, status, alarms) from the wind power plant.
*   Transfer of control commands and set points to the wind power plant.
*   Support for system management functions (e.g., configuration, time synchronization).

**Key Constraints**:
*   The system must be based on open, widely accepted methods and interfaces.
*   Faults in the communication system must not cause malfunction of an individual wind turbine.
*   Time-critical function data must have an overall transfer time not exceeding 0.5 seconds.
