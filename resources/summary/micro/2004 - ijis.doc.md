**Purpose & Scope**: A system to manage the appointment, tracking, and compensation of court-appointed attorneys for indigent defendants in Tarrant County, Texas.

**Core Functions**:
* Process requests for appointed counsel, including indigency determination and attorney assignment.
* Manage attorney lists, qualifications, and appointment rotations.
* Generate compliance reports for the Texas Fair Defense Act.

**Key Constraints**:
* Must comply with specified articles of the Texas Code of Criminal Procedure and Texas Government Code.
* Must integrate with an external Court Case Management System.
* Must adhere to Texas Department of Information Resources guidelines for electronic transactions and records.
