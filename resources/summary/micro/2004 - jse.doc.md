**Purpose & Scope**: Provide an electronic system for the JSE, as the National Numbering Agent, to issue, cancel, and de-register ISIN codes for dematerialized Money Market Instruments upon request from STRATE.

**Core Functions**:
*   Automatically issue a new ISIN in response to a validated SWIFT request.
*   Cancel or de-register an existing ISIN in response to a validated SWIFT request.
*   Validate incoming SWIFT message format and content, sending rejections for failures.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must use SWIFT MT598 message formats as defined in the appendices.
*   Must assign ISINs with the prefix 'ZAM'.
*   Must comply with ANNA and ISO rules for ISIN issuance.
