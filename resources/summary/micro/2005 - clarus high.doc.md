**Purpose & Scope**
The Clarus system is a nationwide network to collect, quality control, and disseminate surface transportation environmental data (atmospheric, pavement, hydrologic) from multiple sources across North America.

**Core Functions**
*   Collect environmental observations from approved data providers.
*   Apply continuous quality control processes and flag data.
*   Disseminate quality-controlled data to service providers and other users.

**Key Constraints**
*   The system must collect data from sources within 5 minutes of availability and publish new data within 20 minutes of receipt.
*   The system must operate on an open, standards-based architecture using non-proprietary interfaces.
*   The system must support continuous 24x7 operations.
