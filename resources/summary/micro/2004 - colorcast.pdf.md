**Purpose & Scope**: Enable a smooth transition for ABC Paint customers from an old to a new paint numbering scheme via a web-based application integrated into the company website.

**Core Functions**:
* Translate old paint numbers to new scheme numbers.
* Search for paints by name, number, or color value.
* Allow graphical color selection with a pointing device.

**Key Constraints**:
* Must be a web-based client application.
* Must be operational by the second quarter of 2004.
* Server must process color search queries in sub-second time.
