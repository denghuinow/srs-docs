**Purpose & Scope**: Enable cross-border electronic submission of company qualification documents for public procurement, mapping national attestations to EU selection/exclusion criteria.

**Core Functions**:
*   Provide a pre-VCD mapping tool for matching national attestations to EU criteria.
*   Compile a VCD simple package containing required evidences and metadata.
*   Support creation of advanced VCD packages with structured, machine-interpretable data.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must align with Directive 2004/18/EC Articles 45-50 for selection/exclusion criteria.
*   Requires mutual recognition of attestations across Member States.
*   Dependent on establishing trusted national VCD service providers.
