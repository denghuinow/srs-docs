**Purpose & Scope**: A system to support public health investigation, monitoring, management, analysis, and reporting of outbreaks and other health events.

**Core Functions**:
*   Collect and link data for cases, exposures, contacts, specimens, and treatments.
*   Trace contact exposures and define relationships between all investigation entities.
*   Exchange investigation data with partner systems using established standards.

**Key Constraints**:
*   The system must be configurable to support different event types and allow dynamic definition of data fields and entities.
*   Data exchange with other public health systems is required.
*   System and data security, availability, and privacy protections are required.
