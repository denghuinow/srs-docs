**Purpose & Scope:**  
A digital radio system based on GSM, providing interoperable ground-train and ground-based mobile voice and data communications for European railways.

**Core Functions:**  
- Voice group and broadcast calls for operational communications.  
- Functional and location-dependent addressing (e.g., call by train number, route to local controller).  
- Railway emergency calls with high priority and confirmation.

**Key Constraints:**  
- Must operate in the 876–880/921–925 MHz frequency band (R-GSM).  
- Network must support mandatory GSM services (e.g., VGCS, VBS, eMLPP) and railway-specific applications.  
- Mobile equipment (Cab, General Purpose, Operational radios) must meet defined environmental, power, and interface standards.
