**Purpose & Scope**: The Clarus system collects, quality checks, and disseminates surface transportation environmental data (atmospheric, pavement, hydrologic) from across North America to enhance safety, mobility, and weather forecasting.

**Core Functions**:
*   Collect environmental data from diverse, autonomous sensor networks and contributors.
*   Apply automated and manual quality checking processes to all incoming data.
*   Disseminate qualified environmental data and metadata to authorized service providers and users.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Data dissemination is restricted by source-specific data sharing agreements.
*   The system must operate continuously (24x7).
*   The system architecture must be open and standards-based (e.g., NTCIP 1204).
