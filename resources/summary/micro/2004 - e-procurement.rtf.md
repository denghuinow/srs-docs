**Purpose & Scope**: Defines interoperability requirements for electronic procurement document exchange between public sector buyers and external sellers, covering the cycle from catalogue to remittance.

**Core Functions**:
*   Exchange standardized electronic documents (e.g., Purchase Order, Invoice) between buyer and seller systems.
*   Uniquely identify and link documents, parties, and items across the procurement cycle.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Documents must be implemented as XML and comply with the e-Government Interoperability Framework (e-GIF).
*   Mandatory use of a status attribute (original, copy, revision, cancellation) in all documents.
*   Reliance on standardized international and national codes for parties, items, and currencies.
