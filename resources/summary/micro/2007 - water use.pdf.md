**Purpose & Scope**: A GIS-based system for Southwest Florida Water Management District to spatially and temporally track and analyze regulatory and resource management data, primarily to support the Southern Water Use Caution Area (SWUCA) Management Plan.

**Core Functions**:
*   View water use permit information and search for permits.
*   View and analyze data spatially using maps.
*   Generate reports on water use, compliance, and trends.

**Key Constraints**:
*   The system must integrate data from existing DB2 (Regulatory, Water Management) and ArcSDE/Oracle (GIS) databases.
*   The system must operate within the District's current hardware and software architecture.
*   Required data not currently in source databases may necessitate changes outside this project's scope.
