**Purpose & Scope**
The system provides an automated electronic method for the JSE, as the National Numbering Agent (NNA), to issue, cancel, and de-register ISIN codes for dematerialized Money Market Instruments (MMIs). It enables same-day trading and settlement. The system does not handle equities or bonds, nor does it manage the underlying trading or settlement processes.

**Product Background / Positioning**
The system is a core component of the South African electronic money market initiative led by the Money Market Forum (MMF). It interfaces exclusively with STRATE (the Central Securities Depository) to support STRATE's SAFIRES settlement system. The JSE operates this service as the sole authorized NNA for the country.

**Core Functional Overview**
*   Automatically issue a new ISIN in response to a validated electronic request from STRATE.
*   Cancel/de-issue an existing ISIN upon validated request from STRATE.
*   De-register an ISIN upon instrument maturity via automated request from STRATE.
*   Validate incoming SWIFT message format and content.
*   Generate and send confirmations or rejections for all request types.
*   Maintain a master database of issued, cancelled, and matured ISINs.
*   Provide a front-end interface for JSE staff to monitor, query, and manually manage ISINs.

**Key Users & Usage Scenarios**
*   **STRATE's MMI Engine:** The primary external user. It automatically sends ISIN creation, cancellation, and maturity de-registration requests and receives confirmations.
*   **JSE Clearing & Settlement Staff:** Internal users. They monitor the automated process, handle rejections, perform manual ISIN entries during Business Continuity Plan (BCP) events, and query the ISIN database via the front-end.

**Major External Interfaces**
The sole external interface is a SWIFT-based messaging link using MT598 (ISO15022) proprietary messages between the JSE and STRATE. A new JSE BIC branch code (XJSEZAJJXISN) is used specifically for this communication.

**Key Non-functional Requirements**
*   **Performance:** Must support an estimated volume of 200 to 500 ISIN issuances per day.
*   **Availability/Reliability:** Requires a defined Business Continuity Plan (BCP) with manual override capabilities.
*   **Security:** Must comply with ANNA and ISO 6166 rules for ISIN issuance. SWIFT communication protocol is mandated.
*   **Maintainability:** A Service Level Agreement (SLA) governing administration, maintenance, and support must be established with STRATE.

**Constraints, Assumptions & Dependencies**
*   The ISIN prefix for MMIs is fixed as "ZAM".
*   System must adhere to ANNA guidelines and ISO 6166 standards.
*   Message formats and contents are dependent on final approval from the MMF and the National Market Practice Group (NMPG).
*   All costs for development and operation are to be recovered from STRATE.
*   The system is scheduled for implementation in Q2 2004.

**Priorities & Acceptance Approach**
The highest priority is the reliable, automated issuance of a new ISIN in response to a valid SWIFT request, as this enables immediate trading. Acceptance will be based on successful end-to-end testing of the nine defined SWIFT message flows (request, confirmation, and rejection for Issuance, Cancellation, and Maturity De-registration) between STRATE and the JSE system.
