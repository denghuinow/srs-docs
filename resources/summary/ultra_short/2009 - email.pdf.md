**Purpose & Scope**
The system is a statewide enterprise email, messaging, and calendaring service for all executive branch agencies. Its purpose is to reduce operational costs and consolidate existing agency systems. It does not include collaboration services like shared documents, instant messaging, or discussion forums.

**Product Background / Positioning**
This is a mandated enterprise IT service to replace the current disparate email systems used by individual state agencies. It will be a centralized service, and the output of this project is a proposed plan analyzing sourcing options (in-house, external, or hybrid) for its delivery.

**Core Functional Overview**
*   Send, receive, and manage email with attachments.
*   Manage calendars, contacts, and distribution lists.
*   Provide server-based archiving, retention, and legal discovery capabilities.
*   Implement security, anti-virus, and content filtering.
*   Enable remote and mobile device access (e.g., web, BlackBerry, other devices).
*   Perform backup and restore operations at multiple levels (message, mailbox, server).
*   Allow agency administrators to provision and manage user accounts.
*   Support integration with agency applications via standard protocols (e.g., SMTP).

**Key Users & Usage Scenarios**
Primary users are agency employees sending and receiving email. Agency administrators manage accounts, distribution lists, and archiving. Data center/SSRC administrators manage system-wide security, backups, and message routing. Typical scenarios include daily email communication, scheduling meetings, searching archived messages for legal discovery, and accessing email from mobile devices.

**Major External Interfaces**
The system must interface with existing agency LDAP-like directory services for account provisioning. It must support integration with agency software applications. External interfaces include internet email and secure access for remote/mobile users.

**Key Non-functional Requirements**
The system must meet federal and state requirements for confidentiality, privacy, and security. It must provide a disaster recovery capability. The archiving system must comply with legal retention requirements (e.g., Sarbanes-Oxley, HIPAA, Florida Public Records Law).

**Constraints, Assumptions & Dependencies**
The final plan must be submitted by December 31, 2009. Migration of all agencies must be scheduled to complete by June 30, 2013. The solution depends on reusing or integrating with existing agency directory services and applications. All requirements are subject to technical and financial feasibility analysis.

**Priorities & Acceptance Approach**
"Basic" functional requirements represent the mandatory minimal solution. "Extended" requirements are optional capabilities that may incur additional cost. Acceptance will be based on the proposed plan's ability to meet the basic requirements and its included cost-benefit analysis of sourcing options.
