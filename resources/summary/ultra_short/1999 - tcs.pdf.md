**Purpose & Scope**
The system provides a single command, control, data receipt, processing, and dissemination system for tactical unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). It is interoperable with present and future tactical UAVs, including the Outrider and Predator, their payloads, and network communication systems. It does not include the design of the UAVs or payloads themselves, only their control and data interface.

**Product Background / Positioning**
The Tactical Control System (TCS) is a joint-service program to replace and unify separate UAV ground control stations. It is designed to integrate into the existing Joint C4I (Command, Control, Communications, Computers, and Intelligence) infrastructure, connecting to numerous specified battlefield management, fire control, and intelligence systems. Its development is phased, with the goal of establishing a common software architecture for all future tactical UAVs.

**Core Functional Overview**
1.  **Mission Planning:** Generate, receive, process, store, and transmit UAV mission plans, including flight routes, payload tasks, and communications.
2.  **Mission Control & Monitoring:** Command and monitor the flight, navigation, and status of UAVs, their payloads (EO/IR, SAR), and data links.
3.  **Payload Product Management:** Receive, process, display, annotate, enhance, store, and disseminate payload imagery and data.
4.  **Targeting:** Determine target coordinates from payload imagery and estimate the accuracy of those coordinates.
5.  **C4I System Interface:** Exchange tactical messages, mission plans, and imagery (digital and analog) with a long list of specified external command and control systems.
6.  **System Maintenance & Diagnostics:** Perform fault detection, location, and software upgrades for TCS components and interfaced systems.

**Key Users & Usage Scenarios**
Primary users are military UAV operators and maintainers. Operators conduct mission planning, execute flights, control sensors, and disseminate intelligence. Maintainers perform system diagnostics and software updates. The system supports distinct operational modes: Normal Operations, Training Operations, and Maintenance Operations. A single operator must be able to control two Medium Altitude Endurance UAVs simultaneously.

**Major External Interfaces**
Interfaces exist to: UAVs and their data links; a long list of over 20 specified C4I systems (e.g., AFATDS, JSTARS, ASAS); external data storage; printers; external power supplies; and sources of geospatial data. Communications use tactical radios, satellite links, and local area networks.

**Key Non-functional Requirements**
*   **Performance:** Startup within 60 seconds (normal) or 45 seconds (recovery). Operator command to system acknowledgment within 1 second. Data latency not greater than existing Predator or Outrider ground stations.
*   **Reliability & Availability:** Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) and Availability (Ao) equal to or greater than thresholds specified in the Predator and Outrider Operational Requirements Documents.
*   **Safety:** No single hardware or software failure shall result in an unsafe command being transmitted to and accepted by the air vehicle.
*   **Security:** Must meet DoD security requirements for automated information systems, including computer, communications, and physical security, accredited for processing classified data.
*   **Resource Utilization:** Processor throughput shall not exceed 50% of capability over any 10-second period. A 50% spare memory storage capacity is required.
*   **Environmental:** Must operate in worldwide climatic conditions specified for land-based shelters and shipboard environments.
*   **Endurance:** Capable of continuous operation for a minimum of 72 hours.

**Constraints, Assumptions & Dependencies**
*   **Compliance:** Must comply with Defense Information Infrastructure/Common Operating Environment (DII/COE), Joint Technical Architecture (JTA), and other listed military/commercial standards (e.g., NITF 2.0, VMF).
*   **Hardware/Software:** To the extent possible, must use standard DoD components, Commercial-Off-The-Shelf (COTS) hardware, and non-proprietary software with unlimited data rights.
*   **External Dependencies:** Functionality is dependent on successful interface development with the numerous listed external C4I systems. Assumes provision of UAV-specific data link and telemetry formats.
*   **Year 2000 Compliance:** All hardware and software configuration items must be Year 2000 compliant.

**Priorities & Acceptance Approach**
All stated requirements are of equal weight unless otherwise identified in a traceability matrix. Conflicting requirements are resolved with precedence given to the TCS Operational Requirements Document (ORD), then this specification, then other requirement documents. Verification will be achieved through an integrated test program including inspections, analyses, demonstrations, and tests as defined in a master test plan.
