**Purpose & Scope**
The system provides an automated method for the JSE, as the National Numbering Agent (NNA), to issue, cancel, and de-register ISIN codes for dematerialized money market instruments. The scope is limited to the electronic exchange of SWIFT messages between STRATE and the JSE for these lifecycle events.

**Core Functions**
*   Automatically issue a new ISIN in response to a valid electronic request.
*   Cancel or de-issue an existing ISIN upon request.
*   De-register an ISIN for an instrument that has reached maturity.

**Key Users**
*   STRATE (as the central securities depository and requestor).
*   JSE (as the National Numbering Agent and service provider).

**Key Constraints**
*   Communication must use specific, pre-defined SWIFT MT598 message formats.
*   ISIN issuance must comply with ANNA and ISO rules.
*   The system must handle an estimated volume of 200 to 500 ISIN issuances per day.
