**Purpose & Scope**
The system defines functional requirements for a European Train Control System (ETCS) to enable safe train driving by supervising train and shunting movements. It operates up to a maximum train speed of 500 km/h and must be compatible with existing national train control systems.

**Core Functions**
*   Supervise train speed and movement authorities, including emergency and service brake intervention.
*   Operate in defined supervision states (e.g., Full, Partial, Shunting, On Sight).
*   Execute an emergency stop command from the trackside.
*   Manage train data entry and verification before movement.
*   Calculate and supervise braking curves based on train and infrastructure data.

**Key Users**
*   Train Driver.
*   Infrastructure Operator (e.g., Radio Block Centre - RBC).

**Key Constraints**
*   Mandatory (M) requirements shall be respected in every application.
*   Must not interfere with, and not be interfered by, compatible national systems.
*   Onboard equipment must be capable of supervising a defined set of operational states.
*   National values for adaptation to local rules must be supported.
