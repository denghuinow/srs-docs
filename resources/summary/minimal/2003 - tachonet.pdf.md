**Purpose & Scope**: The system enables secure, standardized exchange of driver smart card and tachograph card information between European Member States' Card Issuing Authorities to prevent fraud and manage card lifecycles.

**Core Functions**:
*   Check driver's issued cards across Member States.
*   Check tachograph card status.
*   Declare card status modifications (e.g., lost, stolen).
*   Generate and browse system usage statistics.
*   Compute phonetic search keys for name matching.

**Key Users**: Card Issuing Authority (CIA) Administrators, CIA Applications (as system interfaces), TCN Administrators.

**Key Constraints**: Must guarantee non-repudiation and data privacy for all transactions. The system's design must prevent any Member State or administrator from reconstructing a consolidated European database. Must operate on the TESTA-II network. Must respond to user requests in less than one minute and operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
