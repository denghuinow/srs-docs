**Purpose & Scope**: The system is a website to attract Oregon high school students to pursue computer science degrees at Oregon University System campuses.

**Core Functions**:
*   Provide information on CS programs at Oregon universities.
*   Provide career and salary information for CS graduates.
*   Showcase profiles of real people in diverse CS careers.

**Key Users**: Oregon high school students (grades 9-12), including those interested in CS and those potentially interested.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must operate within the OUS domain and on OSU servers.
*   Must support IE, Firefox, and Netscape browsers.
*   Limited by available server space, bandwidth for video, and Chancellor's Office resources.
