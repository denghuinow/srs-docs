**Purpose & Scope**
The system supports the investigation, monitoring, management, analysis, and reporting of public health outbreaks and bioterrorism events to aid in containment. It is configurable for different event types and integrates with other public health systems.

**Core Functions**
*   Conduct case investigations using configurable electronic questionnaires.
*   Create and maintain traceable links between all entities involved in an event.
*   Perform contact exposure tracing and monitoring.
*   Generate analysis, visualizations, and reports.
*   Exchange data with external laboratory, surveillance, and response systems.

**Key Users**
Public health investigators and response staff.

**Key Constraints**
*   The system architecture must be configurable to add new data fields and entity types per event.
*   Data exchange with partner systems must use established vocabulary and messaging standards.
*   The system must protect data from unauthorized access and corruption.
*   Patient and organizational privacy must be protected.
*   A continuity plan is required for system availability.
