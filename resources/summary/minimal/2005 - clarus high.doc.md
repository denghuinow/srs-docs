**Purpose & Scope**
The system collects, quality controls, and disseminates surface transportation environmental and road condition observations for North America to improve safety, mobility, and weather forecasting.

**Core Functions**
*   Collect in-situ and remotely sensed environmental observations from approved data providers.
*   Perform continuous, automated quality control on collected data and attach quality flags.
*   Publish quality-controlled data and metadata for access by service providers and other users.

**Key Users**
*   Data Providers (e.g., transportation agencies, rail operators, vehicle fleets).
*   Data Consumers (e.g., weather service providers, transportation maintenance and operations personnel, researchers).

**Key Constraints**
*   The system architecture must be open and standards-based.
*   The system must collect data from sources within 5 minutes of availability and publish new data within 20 minutes of receipt.
*   The system must operate continuously (24x7).
