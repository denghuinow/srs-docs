**Purpose & Scope**: The system ensures electric grid restoration after a complete or major blackout by planning for and managing generators that can self-start without off-site power.

**Core Functions**:
*   Determine required quantity and location of Black Start generators through contingency studies.
*   Annually verify and contract for sufficient Black Start generator capacity.
*   Test Black Start generators to validate their self-start capability and performance.
*   Maintain a database of all designated Black Start generators.

**Key Users**: CAISO (system operator), generator owners/operators, neighboring balancing authorities.

**Key Constraints**: The plan is reviewed and updated at least every five years. Black Start units must meet specific start-up time limits (e.g., 30 minutes for hydro/gas turbines). A unit must achieve at least 99% of requested output during a four-hour availability test to be declared fully available.
