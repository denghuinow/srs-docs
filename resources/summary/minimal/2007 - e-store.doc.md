**Purpose & Scope**
The system is an online store for selling electronics and home entertainment products. It enables online sales, distribution, and marketing.

**Core Functions**
*   Online purchase of products.
*   Shopping cart management.
*   Support for multiple payment methods.
*   Product search and browsing with detailed categorization.
*   Customer profile creation and management.

**Key Users**
*   Customers.
*   Administrators.

**Key Constraints**
*   The product must be web-based.
*   Data transfer must use secure sockets for confidential information.
*   The system must interface with external systems for payment processing, tax calculation, shipping, and credit management.
