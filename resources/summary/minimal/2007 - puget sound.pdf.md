**Purpose & Scope**: The system enhances the Moodle courseware platform to replace Blackboard and meet University of Puget Sound instructional needs, utilizing existing Moodle APIs.

**Core Functions**:
*   Enable configurable multiple file uploads per course page.
*   Capture, organize, and manage student audio recordings in a portfolio.
*   Provide course-wide search functionality from any page.
*   Allow course administrators to grade assignments and maintain a grade history.
*   Deliver email and SMS notifications for page updates.

**Key Users**: Students, Professors, System Administrators.

**Key Constraints**:
*   Must support at least 1000 concurrent users.
*   Must be backed up on a configurable schedule with restores possible within six hours.
*   Enhancements must utilize the existing Moodle APIs.
