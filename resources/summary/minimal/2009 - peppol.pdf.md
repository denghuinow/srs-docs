**Purpose & Scope**
The system enables economic operators to electronically assemble and submit qualification documents required for EU public procurement to any contracting authority across member states, based on criteria from Directives 2004/18/EC and 2004/17/EC.

**Core Functions**
*   Maps national qualification documents to EU-wide selection/exclusion criteria.
*   Compiles a structured electronic package (VCD) containing required documents and metadata.
*   Processes context-specific data from documents for automated decision support.

**Key Users**
*   Economic Operators (bidders).
*   Contracting Authorities (public buyers).
*   Issuing Bodies (document providers).
*   VCD Service Providers.

**Key Constraints**
*   Must comply with EU public procurement directives and national implementations.
*   Requires mutual recognition of documents and procedures across member states.
*   Depends on achieving semantic interoperability for document content.
