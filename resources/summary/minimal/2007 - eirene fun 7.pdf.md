**Purpose & Scope:** The system provides interoperable mobile voice and data communications for European railways based on GSM-R technology, primarily to ensure cross-border interoperability on international lines and to replace national systems.

**Core Functions:**
*   Initiate and manage high-priority Railway Emergency Calls (train and shunting emergencies).
*   Support voice communications (point-to-point, group, broadcast) between drivers, controllers, and operational staff.
*   Provide a dedicated shunting mode with a link assurance signal for safety-critical manoeuvres.
*   Enable functional and location-dependent addressing (e.g., call a driver by train number).
*   Bear data for external applications, specifically the ERTMS/ETCS train control system.

**Key Users:** Train drivers (using Cab Radio), railway controllers (Primary, Secondary), and operational staff (e.g., shunting teams, trackside workers using Operational Radio).

**Key Constraints:**
*   The system and mobile equipment must comply with mandatory (M) requirements for interoperability.
*   Call setup times for Railway Emergency Calls must be under 2 seconds in 95% of cases.
*   Network coverage must support mobile speeds up to 500 km/h.
*   All mobile equipment must function within the designated railway frequency bands around 900 MHz.
