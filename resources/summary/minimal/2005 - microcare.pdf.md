**Purpose & Scope**: The system automates the management of a voucher-based STD treatment program in Uganda's Mbarara district, handling the voucher lifecycle from creation to provider reimbursement.

**Core Functions**:
*   Create and track uniquely identified, barcoded vouchers.
*   Manage voucher distribution and sales through a network of distributors.
*   Process and validate treatment claims submitted by healthcare providers.
*   Generate standard financial, medical, and operational reports.

**Key Users**: MSIU administrative staff, voucher distributors, and approved healthcare service providers (VSPs).

**Key Constraints**: The system must interface with barcode and thumb-print readers. Voucher creation and edits are restricted to authorized users. The database design must support scaling beyond an initial 20,000 vouchers.
