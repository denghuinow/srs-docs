**Purpose & Scope**: Defines functional requirements for interoperable electronic procurement message exchange between public sector buyers and external sellers, covering the cycle from catalogue to remittance. It excludes seller discovery, auctions, tendering, and message transport/security.

**Core Functions**:
*   Exchange standardised Purchase Orders and corresponding responses.
*   Exchange Invoices, Credit Notes, Debit Notes, and Remittance Advice for payment.
*   Exchange Catalogues with item and price information.
*   Exchange Requests for Quotation and Quotations.

**Key Users**: Public sector Buyer organizations and external Seller organizations.

**Key Constraints**: Messages must be implemented as XML documents compliant with the e-Government Interoperability Framework (e-GIF). Internal system designs of buyers and sellers are treated as black boxes.
