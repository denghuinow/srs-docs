**Purpose & Scope**: The system collects, quality checks, and disseminates surface transportation weather and road condition observations (atmospheric, pavement, and hydrologic data) across North America.

**Core Functions**:
*   Collect environmental data from multiple, autonomous contributor networks.
*   Apply automated and manual quality checks to environmental data.
*   Disseminate qualified environmental data and metadata to service providers and other users.
*   Manage user subscriptions for data delivery.
*   Restrict data publication based on contributor agreements.

**Key Users**: Data contributors (e.g., transportation agencies), weather service providers, and transportation operations/maintenance personnel.

**Key Constraints**: The system must only accept observations that include location, timestamp, and source metadata. It must use industry-standard, non-proprietary interfaces and adhere to defined government IT security requirements. It must respond to environmental data requests within one minute.
