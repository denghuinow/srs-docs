**Purpose & Scope**: The software automates fault detection and diagnosis in HVAC subsystems (chillers and cooling towers) using pattern-recognition algorithms on sensed and fixed data, replacing manual expert analysis.

**Core Functions**:
*   Detect and identify specific performance and operational faults in chiller and cooling tower subsystems.
*   Input and validate sensed data (e.g., temperatures, currents) from real-time devices or batch files.
*   Continuously monitor the unit and log diagnostic results with associated fault descriptions, causes, and recommended fixes.
*   Provide a hierarchical user interface to browse current and historical diagnostic results.

**Key Users**: Building operators, HVAC service technicians, energy service providers, and building operator supervisors.

**Key Constraints**: The software is restricted to Microsoft Windows-compatible operating systems. Its design must support the incorporation of diagnostic algorithms for additional HVAC subsystems in the future.
