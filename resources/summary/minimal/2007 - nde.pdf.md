**Purpose & Scope**: The system processes, archives, and distributes environmental satellite data products from the NPP and NPOESS satellite missions to authorized users. It receives data from the Interface Data Processing Segment (IDPS), generates tailored and NOAA-unique products, and interfaces with the CLASS archive.

**Core Functions**:
*   Receive and ingest all data records and telemetry from the IDPS.
*   Generate NOAA-unique and tailored data products, including format conversion, compression, and projection.
*   Distribute data products to registered users and deliver NOAA-unique products to the CLASS long-term archive.
*   Operate in defined modes: Open, Degraded System, and Restricted Access.
*   Monitor and report on input data integrity, output product quality, and system health.

**Key Users**: NOAA operational and climate communities; other authorized civilian customers.

**Key Constraints**:
*   System availability: Core capabilities (Ingest, Product Generation, Distribution, Operations Monitoring) shall not be interrupted for more than 2 hours in any 24-hour period.
*   Data latency: Products must be made available for distribution within 5 minutes after all required input data is received.
*   Capacity: Must support input/output volumes of 4/8 TB per day during NPP, scaling to 8/16 TB per day for NPOESS missions.
