**Purpose & Scope**
The system is a web-based Supply Chain Management (SCM) application for Ejada company to deliver products and services. Its scope is limited to Ejada's operations.

**Core Functions**
*   Manage requests from customers and to suppliers.
*   Manage items (products) for supply.
*   Manage customer and supplier entity information.

**Key Users**
Ejada employees (Coordinators), Customers, and Suppliers.

**Key Constraints**
The system must be web-based, built using .NET technologies (ASP.NET, C#, MS SQL Server), and integrate with Ejada's existing framework.
