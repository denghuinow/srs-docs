**Purpose & Scope**: The system defines a digital radio standard based on GSM to ensure mobile communications interoperability for European railways. It covers ground-to-train voice and data communications and the mobile communication needs of trackside, station, depot, and administrative personnel.

**Core Functions**:
*   Provide mandatory GSM voice services, including telephony, Voice Group Call Service (VGCS), and Voice Broadcast Service (VBS).
*   Support railway-specific services: functional addressing, location-dependent addressing, and high-priority railway emergency calls.
*   Enable interoperable communications for trains and staff crossing national borders between compliant systems.

**Key Users**: Train drivers (using Cab Radio), railway controllers, and operational staff (e.g., shunting teams, maintenance workers using General Purpose or Operational Radios).

**Key Constraints**:
*   The network and mobile equipment must comply with specified mandatory GSM services and the EIRENE SRS for interoperability.
*   The system must operate in the designated Railway-GSM (R-GSM) frequency band (876-915 MHz mobile transmit / 921-960 MHz base transmit).
*   Network coverage must meet defined minimum field strength levels for voice, data, and train control (ETCS) services.
