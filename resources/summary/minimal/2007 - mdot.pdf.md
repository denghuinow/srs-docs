**Purpose & Scope**: The system is a research prototype to investigate and evaluate the utility of Vehicle Infrastructure Integration (VII) probe data, integrated with other transportation data sources, for enhancing safety, mobility, and asset management. Its scope is the acquisition, processing, storage, and dissemination of this data for demonstration and assessment.

**Core Functions**:
*   Collect probe vehicle, traffic, weather, and road condition data from multiple external sources.
*   Process incoming data through computational algorithms to derive information like traffic speed, incidents, travel times, and road surface conditions.
*   Publish processed data and alerts in standardized formats for use by external systems and internal presentation services.

**Key Users**: Transportation agency operators, maintainers, and asset managers; system administrators; travelers; emergency services; researchers.

**Key Constraints**:
*   The system must handle data that is "near real-time" and assess its suitability based on source and timestamps.
*   It must conform to IT standards established by the Michigan Department of Information Technology.
*   Operational policies are required to address data privacy and ownership issues arising from the use of probe vehicle data.
