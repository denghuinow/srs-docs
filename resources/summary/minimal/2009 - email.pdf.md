**Purpose & Scope**: The system is a statewide enterprise email, messaging, and calendaring service for all executive branch agencies, designed to reduce operational costs and replace existing agency systems.

**Core Functions**:
*   Send, receive, and manage email with attachments.
*   Provide calendaring, scheduling, and contact management.
*   Archive, retain, and enable discovery of emails for legal and regulatory compliance.
*   Provide antivirus protection and content filtering.
*   Enable remote and mobile device access to email.

**Key Users**: End-users, Agency Administrators, Data Center/System Administrators.

**Key Constraints**: A plan for the system must be submitted by December 31, 2009. The solution must reduce costs and meet state and federal confidentiality, privacy, and security requirements.
