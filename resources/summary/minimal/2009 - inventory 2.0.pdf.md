**Purpose & Scope**: The system is a categorized inventory management application for Construction Junction that tracks items from donation through sale. It integrates with QuickBooks Point of Sale and the organization's CRM/website.

**Core Functions**:
*   View and navigate a categorized inventory hierarchy.
*   Add items to inventory, primarily through processing donor acquisitions.
*   Receive and process donor acquisitions, including printing donation receipts.
*   Update inventory to reflect item sales.
*   Manage (modify, adjust quantity, split) existing inventory items.

**Key Users**: Construction Junction staff (Administrator, Director, Manager, Receiving Associate, Sales Associate, Pickup/Decon Associate, Customer Service Representative), Donors, and Buyers.

**Key Constraints**: The system must integrate with QuickBooks Point of Sale and Salesforce CRM. It must support touch-screen workstations and barcode scanners for core operations.
