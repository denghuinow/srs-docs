**Purpose & Scope**
The system provides a unified command, control, and data processing solution for multiple tactical unmanned aerial vehicles. Its scope includes control of the Predator and Outrider UAVs, interoperability with specified C4I systems, and support for five defined levels of UAV interaction.

**Core Functions**
*   Mission Planning.
*   Mission Control and Monitoring.
*   Payload Product Management.
*   C4I System Interface.

**Key Users**
UAV operators, mission planners, and maintenance personnel.

**Key Constraints**
*   Must be compliant with the Defense Information Infrastructure/Common Operating Environment.
*   Must interface with a specified list of Command, Control, Communications, Computers, and Intelligence systems.
*   Must operate within defined states (Startup, Operations, Shutdown) with stipulated mode transition times.
