**Purpose & Scope**: The system is a digital repository to preserve and provide long-term access to the National Library of Medicine's digital biomedical collections, including both digitized and born-digital materials. Its scope is limited to collection materials under Library Operations responsibility.

**Core Functions**:
*   Ingest digital materials and associated metadata via multiple streams.
*   Provide reliable, managed archival storage for digital objects.
*   Enable search, retrieval, and delivery of objects to users with access controls.

**Key Users**: General public and NIH staff (end-users); NLM staff (metadata and object maintenance); NLM system administrators.

**Key Constraints**: Must interoperate with existing NLM digital repositories (e.g., PubMed Central). Must interface with NLM's Integrated Library System for bibliographic data. Must comply with HHS/NIH security standards and the NIH off-site backup facility.
