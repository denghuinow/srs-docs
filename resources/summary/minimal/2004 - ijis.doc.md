**Purpose & Scope**: The system manages the appointment, tracking, and compensation of court-appointed defense attorneys for indigent defendants within Tarrant County's criminal justice process.

**Core Functions**:
* Submit and electronically process requests for appointed counsel.
* Manage qualified attorney lists and appointment rotations.
* Track defense attorney contacts with defendants and case events.
* Process and approve defense attorney compensation claims.
* Generate compliance reports for the Texas Fair Defense Act.

**Key Users**: Defendant, Magistrate, Defense Attorney, Office of Attorney Appointments.

**Key Constraints**: Must comply with specified Texas Code of Criminal Procedure articles and Texas Government Code. Must integrate with external Court Case Management System.
