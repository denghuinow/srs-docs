**Short Summary**

**Background and Objectives**  
The PEPPOL Virtual Company Dossier (VCD) aims to enable economic operators across Europe to electronically submit standardized company information (e.g., certificates, attestations) as evidence for selection and exclusion criteria in public procurement, as per Directive 2004/18/EC. This will reduce administrative burdens and facilitate cross-border tendering by ensuring interoperability and mutual recognition of documents.

**In Scope**  
- Pre-VCD mapping tool for aligning national attestations with EU criteria.  
- VCD simple package compiling digital evidences with structural metadata.  
- VCD advanced package adding context-specific data and re-compilation services.  
- VCD network package enabling on-demand retrieval of attestations.  
- Legal, organizational, semantic, and technical interoperability frameworks.

**Out of Scope**  
- Full translation of all attestations (selective translation may be used).  
- Mandatory implementation of Article 52 (official lists of approved operators).  
- Complete automation of all evidence collection (manual processes remain).  
- Development of new electronic attestation standards (leverages existing ones).  
- National infrastructure changes (utilizes existing systems where possible).

**Stakeholders and Core Use Cases**  
- **Economic Operators**: Submit VCD packages to prove eligibility.  
- **Contracting Authorities**: Receive and validate VCD packages for tender evaluation.  
- **Issuing Bodies**: Provide attestations and certificates.  
- **National VCD Service Providers**: Host VCD systems and ensure trust.  
- **European Service Provider**: Maintains the pre-VCD mapping tool.  
- **Translators**: Provide certified translations of evidences when required.

**User Stories**  
1. As an economic operator, I want to map national attestations to EU criteria so that I can prove compliance in cross-border tenders.  
2. As a contracting authority, I want to receive a structured VCD package so that I can efficiently verify an operator’s suitability.  
3. As an issuing body, I want to provide attestations electronically so that they can be integrated into VCD packages.  
4. As a national VCD service provider, I want to compile VCD packages so that economic operators have a single point of contact.  
5. As a translator, I want to submit certified translations so that foreign attestations are legally acceptable.  
6. As an economic operator, I want to re-compile a VCD for a new tender so that I can reuse valid evidences.

**Success Metrics**  
- Increased cross-border tender participation by economic operators.  
- Reduced time and cost for compiling and verifying eligibility documents.  
- High acceptance and trust in VCD packages among contracting authorities.

**Major Constraints**  
- Diverse national legal frameworks and attestation formats.  
- Limited availability of electronic attestations in some Member States.  
- Need for mutual recognition agreements across borders.  
- Dependence on existing PEPPOL infrastructure (WP8) for secure transport.  
- Language barriers requiring translations or context-specific data.

**Undecided Issues**  
- Governance model for the European pre-VCD mapping tool.  
- Liability of national VCD service providers.  
- Full scope of mutual recognition for foreign procedures under Article 52.  
- Technical approach for context-specific data standardization.  
- Funding and sustainability of VCD services post-pilot.
