# Short Summary: Manage Indigent Defense Services

## Background and Objectives
The Integrated Justice Information System (IJIS) initiative aims to enhance business processes and technology for more effective justice administration in Tarrant County. This specification defines requirements for managing indigent defense services, focusing on efficient appointment and oversight of counsel for defendants who cannot afford an attorney.

## In Scope
- Electronic submission and processing of requests for appointed counsel.
- Management of attorney appointment lists and rotation systems.
- Tracking defense attorney contacts with defendants and case activities.
- Processing and approval of defense attorney compensation claims.
- Generation of compliance reports for the Texas Fair Defense Act.

## Out of Scope
- Detailed design of user interfaces or specific software architecture.
- Management of non-indigent defendant cases.
- Long-term archival and records management beyond active case support.
- Internal financial auditing processes outside compensation approval.
- Training materials or user documentation for the system.

## Stakeholders and Core Use Cases
**Stakeholders:**
- **Defendant:** Person charged with a crime who may request appointed counsel.
- **Magistrate:** Informs defendant of rights and determines indigent status.
- **Office of Attorney Appointments:** Manages the indigent defense appointment process.
- **Defense Attorney:** Represents the defendant after appointment.
- **Judge (County/District):** Approves attorney appointments and compensation.
- **Auditor:** Manages county financial information and processes payments.
- **Court Coordinator:** Manages court schedules and settings.
- **District Attorney:** Represents the state and makes filing decisions.
- **Task Force on Indigent Defense:** Sets standards and receives compliance reports.
- **Detention Facility Officer:** Processes defendants and captures initial information.
- **Commissioners Court:** The county governing body that approves payments.
- **Local Selection Committee:** Qualifies attorneys for capital cases.
- **Presiding Administrative Judge:** Reviews appeals of compensation disputes.
- **Filing Agency:** Law enforcement agency investigating and presenting charges.

**Core User Stories:**
1. As a Magistrate, I want to electronically submit a Request for Appointed Counsel so that the Office of Attorney Appointments can process it promptly.
2. As the Office of Attorney Appointments, I want to select the next attorney from a rotation list so that appointments are made fairly and within legal timeframes.
3. As a Defense Attorney, I want to notify the system of my first contact with a defendant so that compliance with contact requirements is recorded.
4. As a Judge, I want to review and approve defense attorney payment requests so that compensation is processed according to guidelines.
5. As the Office of Attorney Appointments, I want to generate reports on appointment timelines so that compliance with the Texas Fair Defense Act can be demonstrated.
6. As a Defendant, I want to request appointed counsel during magistration so that my right to representation is upheld.

## Success Metrics
- Appointments processed by the end of the first working day after request receipt.
- Defense attorney makes first contact with defendant within one working day of appointment.
- Comprehensive reporting capabilities to demonstrate compliance with state indigent defense standards.

## Major Constraints
- Must comply with Texas Code of Criminal Procedure (CCP) articles governing right to counsel and appointment procedures.
- Must integrate with existing Court Case Management and District Attorney Electronic Case Filing systems.
- Must adhere to Texas Department of Information Resources guidelines for electronic transactions and records.
- Must support reporting requirements specified in Texas Government Code Section 71.0351.
- Must accommodate specific processes for capital felony cases with death penalty sought.

## Undecided Issues
- Specific technical architecture for system integration.
- Detailed data exchange formats with external systems.
- User authentication and access control mechanisms.
- Backup, recovery, and system availability requirements.
- Migration strategy from any existing manual or legacy systems.
