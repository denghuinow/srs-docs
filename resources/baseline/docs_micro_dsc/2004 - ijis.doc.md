### 1. Introduction

#### 1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the Tarrant County Indigent Defense Attorney Management System (TC-IDAMS). The primary purpose of this system is to automate and manage the end-to-end process of appointing, tracking, and compensating court-appointed attorneys for indigent defendants in Tarrant County, Texas. This document is intended for use by stakeholders, project managers, developers, testers, and quality assurance personnel.

#### 1.2 Scope
The TC-IDAMS will be a secure, web-based application that serves judges, court coordinators, county administrators, and approved attorneys. The system's core scope includes:

*   **In-Court Workflow:** Processing requests for appointed counsel, facilitating indigency determinations, and managing attorney assignments during court proceedings.
*   **Attorney Management:** Maintaining a centralized roster of qualified attorneys, including their certifications, areas of practice, and appointment rotation history.
*   **Compliance & Reporting:** Automating the generation of reports required for compliance with the Texas Fair Defense Act (FDA) and other statutory mandates.
*   **Financial Tracking:** Tracking attorney work and expenses to facilitate the compensation approval process.
*   **System Integration:** Exchanging data with the existing external Court Case Management System (CCMS) to ensure consistency of case and party information.

**Out of Scope:**
*   The system will not perform electronic funds transfer (EFT) payments; it will generate payment vouchers for processing by the county's existing financial system.
*   The system will not replace the external CCMS; it is a complementary system for the specific indigent defense appointment workflow.
*   The system will not serve as a public-facing portal for defendants.

### 2. Overall Description

#### 2.1 Product Perspective
TC-IDAMS is a new, self-contained system that must operate within the existing Tarrant County IT ecosystem. It will interface with the external CCMS as a critical partner system.

**System Interfaces:**
*   **CCMS Interface:** A bidirectional, secure API or file-based interface to synchronize case data (case number, defendant information, charges, court dates) and receive updates on case status.
*   **County Active Directory/LDAP:** For user authentication and role-based access control.

**User Interfaces:** The system shall provide a responsive, accessible web interface compatible with modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). Administrative screens will be role-specific.

#### 2.2 Product Functions (High-Level)
1.  **Indigency Determination Support:** Provide forms and calculators to guide the court in making a formal indigency finding as per TCCP.
2.  **Attorney Appointment:** Automate the selection of an attorney from the appropriate qualified rotation list based on case type (e.g., felony, misdemeanor, juvenile, appellate) and court-specific rules.
3.  **Attorney Roster Management:** Allow administrators to add, qualify, suspend, or remove attorneys from appointment lists. Track continuing legal education (CLE) and other certification requirements.
4.  **Work & Expense Logging:** Enable appointed attorneys to submit detailed logs of time spent and expenses incurred, with supporting documentation upload.
5.  **Compliance Reporting:** Generate pre-configured and ad-hoc reports, including FDA-mandated reports on appointment timeliness, attorney distribution, and compensation.
6.  **Audit Trail:** Maintain a complete, immutable log of all critical actions (appointments, status changes, financial entries) for compliance and accountability.

#### 2.3 User Characteristics
| User Class | Characteristics & Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Judge** | Primary actor for making indigency determinations and approving attorney appointments in court. Needs a simple, fast interface. |
| **Court Coordinator / Clerk** | Key operator. Initiates appointment requests, manages the assignment process, inputs court orders, and handles day-to-day system administration for their court. |
| **County Administrator** | Manages system-wide settings, attorney rosters, rotation lists, qualification rules, and user access. Generates and reviews compliance reports. |
| **Attorney** | Views their appointments, updates case status, submits time and expense vouchers for approval, and manages their profile information. |
| **System Auditor** | (Read-only access) Reviews audit trails and generates reports for internal and external (e.g., state) audits. |

#### 2.4 Constraints
1.  **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** The system design and outputs **must** comply with TCCP Articles 1.051, 26.04, 26.05 and relevant TGC chapters. This is a non-negotiable constraint.
2.  **Integration Constraint:** The system **must** integrate with the existing external CCMS. The interface methodology (API, batch file) will be dictated by the CCMS's capabilities.
3.  **Texas DIR Standards:** All electronic transactions, records, and security measures **must** adhere to current Texas DIR guidelines (1 TAC 202).
4.  **Hosting Environment:** The system must be deployable within the Tarrant County approved data center or cloud environment, adhering to county security policies.

#### 2.5 Assumptions and Dependencies
*   **Assumption:** The external CCMS will provide a stable, documented method for data exchange.
*   **Assumption:** County network and identity management infrastructure will be available for integration.
*   **Dependency:** Final system requirements may depend on clarifications from County Legal and Judicial stakeholders regarding specific interpretation of statutory procedures.

### 3. Specific Requirements

#### 3.1 Functional Requirements

**3.1.1 Case & Appointment Management**
*   **FR-1.1:** The system shall allow a Court Coordinator to initiate a request for appointed counsel by searching for or entering a Defendant ID/Case Number synchronized from the CCMS.
*   **FR-1.2:** The system shall provide an electronic "Indigency Determination" form that captures the court's finding as required by TCCP Art. 1.051.
*   **FR-1.3:** Upon a finding of indigency, the system shall automatically present a list of eligible attorneys from the correct rotation list for the case type and court.
*   **FR-1.4:** The system shall allow the Judge or Coordinator to select an attorney from the list, document any conflicts or overrides, and formally appoint the attorney, generating a system record and a printable appointment order.
*   **FR-1.5:** The system shall automatically update the rotation list to move the appointed attorney to the bottom of the applicable list.

**3.1.2 Attorney Roster & List Management**
*   **FR-2.1:** The system shall allow an Administrator to maintain a database of all attorneys eligible for appointment, including contact details, State Bar number, and qualifications.
*   **FR-2.2:** The system shall allow an Administrator to define and manage multiple, separate qualification lists (e.g., Felony Trial, Misdemeanor, Juvenile, Appellate, Capital).
*   **FR-2.3:** The system shall track attorney certification expiration dates (e.g., CLE for death penalty cases) and automatically suspend attorneys from relevant lists upon expiration, notifying the administrator and attorney.
*   **FR-2.4:** The system shall maintain the historical order of attorneys on each rotation list and enforce the rotation logic during appointment.

**3.1.3 Time & Expense Management**
*   **FR-3.1:** The system shall provide a secure portal for Appointed Attorneys to log time entries (date, activity, duration) and itemized expenses for their appointed cases.
*   **FR-3.2:** The system shall allow Attorneys to upload scanned receipts or supporting documentation for expenses.
*   **FR-3.3:** The system shall enforce county-defined maximum hour and expense limits per case type, triggering an alert for submissions that exceed thresholds.
*   **FR-3.4:** The system shall route submitted vouchers to the appropriate Judge or Administrator for review and electronic approval.

**3.1.4 Reporting**
*   **FR-4.1:** The system shall generate the specific reports required by the Texas Fair Defense Act, including but not limited to:
    *   Report on the percentage of indigent defendants receiving appointed counsel within FDA timeframes.
    *   Report on the distribution of appointments among qualified attorneys.
    *   Report on attorney compensation by case type.
*   **FR-4.2:** The system shall allow Administrators to generate ad-hoc reports based on key data fields (court, date range, attorney, case type, appointment status).
*   **FR-4.3:** All reports shall be exportable to PDF and CSV formats.

**3.1.5 System Integration**
*   **FR-5.1:** The system shall initiate a daily (or more frequent) synchronization process with the CCMS to receive new case data and updates.
*   **FR-5.2:** The system shall send appointment and attorney assignment data back to the CCMS to update the case record.
*   **FR-5.3:** The interface shall handle data conflicts with a defined reconciliation process logged for administrator review.

#### 3.2 Non-Functional Requirements

**3.2.1 Security**
*   **NFR-S1:** The system shall implement role-based access control (RBAC) as defined in Section 2.3.
*   **NFR-S2:** All user authentication shall integrate with the county's Active Directory/LDAP system.
*   **NFR-S3:** All Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and case data shall be encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
*   **NFR-S4:** The system shall maintain a tamper-evident audit log of all user actions (who, what, when) related to appointments, financial data, and roster changes, retained for a minimum of 7 years.

**3.2.2 Compliance**
*   **NFR-C1:** The system shall ensure all electronic records and signatures comply with Texas DIR guidelines and are legally admissible.
*   **NFR-C2:** System workflows shall enforce business rules derived from TCCP Articles 26.04 (Appointment of Counsel) and 26.05 (Compensation of Counsel).

**3.2.3 Performance**
*   **NFR-P1:** The appointment selection screen shall load eligible attorney lists within 3 seconds for 95% of transactions under normal load.
*   **NFR-P2:** The system shall support concurrent use by all users across all Tarrant County courts during peak business hours (8:00 AM - 5:00 PM CT).

**3.2.4 Usability**
*   **NFR-U1:** The in-court appointment interface for Judges shall be designed for use on a bench monitor, with large, clear buttons and minimal clicks to complete an appointment.
*   **NFR-U2:** The system shall meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.

**3.2.5 Reliability & Availability**
*   **NFR-R1:** The system shall have 99.5% uptime during court business hours.
*   **NFR-R2:** Full system backups shall occur daily, with transaction logs backed up hourly.
